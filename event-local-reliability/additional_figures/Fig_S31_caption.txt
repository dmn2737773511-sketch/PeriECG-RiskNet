Fig. S31. Matched timing discrimination and fixed-threshold retention costs. a Mean concordance for Global, Event, and Combined within replay quartets containing both failure and nonfailure episodes. Each quartet fixes the clean target, residual source, and residual scale; only the joint residual rotation varies. Concordance compares every failure/nonfailure pair within a quartet, gives ties half credit, and then weights the quartets equally. G1–G4 contain 42, 39, 16, and 19 mixed quartets, respectively; pooled results use all 116 mixed quartets (381 eligible pairs) among 1,024 held-out quartets. b Complete distribution of the paired Event minus Global concordance difference for those 116 quartets. Positive differences favor Event; negative differences identify reversals. c Pooled episode coverage versus the failure fraction among retained episodes when the predicted failure probability is at most 0.05 (circle), 0.10 (square), or 0.20 (triangle). Coverage uses all 4,096 held-out episodes as the denominator; retained failure uses the number of retained episodes. d Source-specific results for Event at probability at most 0.10. Labels give retained failures/retained episodes; each source contains 1,024 held-out episodes. Failure is defined by primary-detector F1 < 0.95. These are descriptive research operating points without threshold optimization. No confidence intervals or p values are shown because replay episodes share input components and the four recording sources belong to one coded series. Source data and plotting code accompany this figure.
