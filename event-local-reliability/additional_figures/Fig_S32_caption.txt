Fig. S32. Continuous annotated ECG and the limits of matched replay reliability. a Complete empirical cumulative distributions of clean-window beat F1 for the unchanged Primary and Amplitude probes across all 192 continuous two-channel windows from 48 MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database records (47 patient groups). The dotted line marks F1 = 0.95; 22 Primary and 17 Amplitude windows fall below it. b Fractions of matched quartets whose failure status changes across the four joint noise rotations. At target/noise ratios of 20, 10, 0, and −5 dB, mixed-quartet counts are 54, 88, 254, and 81 among 1,152 quartets per ratio for all targets (filled bars), and 20, 52, 248, and 74 among 1,020 quartets per ratio for clean-primary-success targets (outlined bars). c Mean within-quartet failure/nonfailure concordance, weighting each mixed quartet equally: 477 mixed quartets for all targets and 394 for the conditional subset. Agreement is an uncalibrated ranking comparator and has the highest matched concordance. d Coverage and failure fractions among retained episodes at predicted failure probability at most 0.10 for Global, Event, and Combined. Filled circles use all 18,432 episodes; open circles select the 16,320 episodes from 170 clean-primary-success windows. Failure is Primary F1 < 0.95. Models were fitted anew in 47 leave-one-patient-group-out folds, with records 201 and 202 grouped together. Every target enters training; the clean-success subset only selects existing out-of-fold results. Six fixed MIT-BIH Noise Stress Test Database noise windows are shared across training and test folds. No threshold optimization, independent-episode confidence intervals, or hypothesis tests are used. Source data and plotting code accompany this figure.
